# AstroTaca Flat Panel quick start

**Windows software 3.2.2 | 16 October 2026**

## Install and connect

1. Close NINA and AstroTaca. Run the **AstroTaca Setup** file supplied with your panel.
2. If prompted, choose **Install ASCOM Platform** and follow its window.
3. Connect USB and the power supply specified for your panel. Keep the cover's path clear.
4. Open **AstroTaca Flat Panel**. Select its **USB port**, choose **Save**, then **Connect**. The port is remembered.
5. If asked to calibrate, follow [Set Closed and Open](README.md#set-closed-and-open) before using the controls.

## Turn on the light

**In the AstroTaca app:** choose **Close**, wait for **Closed**, select a low
brightness and click **Turn on / apply**. Moving the slider alone does not change
the light. Choose **Light off** when finished.

**In NINA:** open **Equipment > Flat Panel**, select **AstroTaca Flat Panel
(ASCOM)** and connect. Use its settings gear if you need to select the port.
Choose **Close**, wait for **Closed**, then enter a low brightness and choose
**Set**. Switch the light off when finished.

Close before requesting light. Opening turns it off. Keep manual controls idle
during automated exposures. Disconnecting only the AstroTaca app leaves NINA connected.

## Use the button

- **Quick tap:** open or close; stop while moving.
- **Hold 1 second, then release:** while Closed, cycle **Off > Dim > Medium > Bright > Off**.
- **Hold 3 seconds:** stop and turn the light off.

Set your levels in **Button settings**, then **Save to panel**. Dim is your
starting brightness. Saved levels work without a computer; powering on keeps the light off.

**Support the cover if it could fall.** The motor releases after movement or
Halt / release. Keep hands clear during powered motion.
